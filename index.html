FILL ALL THE 100 SQUARES!

NEW GAME

HOW TO PLAY:
You must fill all the the squares from 1 to 100 by following two simple rules:
  1. You can start in any cell you want
  2. Next numbers must be put 2 cells on same row/column, or 1 square away diagonally
Green cells will guide you!
